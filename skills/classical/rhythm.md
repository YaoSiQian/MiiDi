# Rhythmic Feel

Flowing 4/4 or 3/4 at 60-168 BPM. Drums are not used in this style: there is no
drum track, no backbeat, and no kit pattern — the pulse is carried by the bass,
the accompaniment figuration, and the melody's own meter.

## Grid Notes

Subdivision policy: melodies move in quarters and eighths with occasional
sixteenths at faster tempos; harmonic-role instruments use broken-chord figures
(alberti-style arpeggios) on eighth-note grids; bass anchors downbeats. No swing
offsets — all onsets sit exactly on integer ticks of the ppq=480 grid. Articulate
phrase endings by leaving rests as gaps rather than shortening note durations.
