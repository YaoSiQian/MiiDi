# Harmony Vocabulary

Functional harmony in major or minor keys. Core symbols: C, Dm, G7, Am, F, E7,
Bm7b5, and cadential colors Gsus4 and Adim.

## Characteristic Progressions

- **Circle of fifths sequence**: C - Am - Dm - G7 - C
- **Minor descent**: Am - Dm - E7 - Am
- **Applied dominant lift**: C - A7 - Dm - Bm7b5 - E7 - Am

## Cadences

Close sections with an authentic cadence (G7 - C) for finality, a plagal cadence
(F - C) for a gentle amen close, or an imperfect cadence resting on the dominant.
Half cadences on G suit mid-phrase pauses; deceptive motion G7 - Am defers the
arrival one more phrase.
