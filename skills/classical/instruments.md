# Instrumentation

| role | GM program | name | register note |
|---|---|---|---|
| lead melody | 40 | Violin | G3-E6, singing upper line |
| harmonic role | 0 | Acoustic Grand Piano | C2-C6, arpeggiated or chordal support |
| bass | 42 | Cello | C2-G3, walking foundation and duet partner |
| inner color | 73 | Flute | C4-C7, dialogue with the violin |

Avoid percussion of any kind — there is no drum track in this style. Avoid brass
fanfares, pop backbeats, and dense vertical stacks; keep textures transparent so
each line can be followed, and let dynamics shape phrases rather than added
layers.
