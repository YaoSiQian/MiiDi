# Instrumentation

| role | GM program | name | register note |
|---|---|---|---|
| melody | 11 | Vibraphone | C4-A5, soft mallet line, lots of space |
| harmony | 4 | Electric Piano 1 (Rhodes) | C3-B4, lush maj7/min7 voicings |
| bass | 33 | Electric Bass (finger) | E1-D3, round low roots |
| color | 89 | Pad 2 (warm) | C3-C4, barely-audible wash |

Avoid busy virtuosity, bright leads, and hard-edged sounds: no distorted guitars,
no staccato synth stabs, no drum fills that wake the listener up. Keep the
texture sparse — if more than four layers sound at once, remove something.
