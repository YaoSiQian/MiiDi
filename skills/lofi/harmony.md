# Harmony Vocabulary

Warm, jazzy color vocabulary: maj7/min7/9 colors over simple roots. Core symbols:
Fmaj7, Em7, Dm7, Cmaj7, Am7, Bm7b5, G7, plus soft colors Emsus4 and Fadd9.

## Characteristic Progressions

- **Downtown loop**: Fmaj7 - Em7 - Dm7 - Cmaj7
- **Rainy turnaround**: Cmaj7 - Am7 - Dm7 - G7
- **Half-cadence drift**: Am7 - Dm7 - Em7

## Cadences

End loops softly on a maj7 chord (Fmaj7 or Cmaj7) instead of a plain triad; let
the last bar breathe with an unresolved Am7. Avoid strong dominant pulls — when
G7 appears, soften it to Gsus4 or step down Em7 - Dm7 to land.
