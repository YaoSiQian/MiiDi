# Instrumentation

| role | GM program | name | register note |
|---|---|---|---|
| lead melody | 81 | Lead 2 (sawtooth) | C4-C6, sits above the hook line |
| comping harmony | 4 | Electric Piano 1 (Rhodes) | C3-C5, mid-register voicings |
| bass | 33 | Electric Bass (finger) | E1-G3, root-and-fifth motion |
| pad support | 89 | Pad 2 (warm) | C3-C4, optional double for chorus lift |

Avoid clutter: no more than one lead voice at a time, keep the bass off the lead's
register, and resist adding solos or virtuosic fills that compete with the vocal-
style hook. Skip orchestral colors and distorted textures; pop clarity comes from
few layers played tightly on the grid.
