# Rhythmic Feel

Swing feel at 110-208 BPM: the pulse rides on a swung 8th-note grid while the
bass walks quarters. Offbeat 8th positions are delayed by the style's swing
offsets (200 ticks) to create the lilt.

## Drum Patterns

One bar = 1920 ticks; grid steps are 16ths (120 ticks each). The hat line here is
ride-ish and sparse — in GM terms hat 42 stands in for the ride cymbal.

```
step:   1e&u2e&u3e&u4eu  (step = tick/120)
kick:   x.......x.......    ticks [0, 960]      (feathered, low accent)
snare:  ....x.......x...    ticks [480, 1440]   (comping accents, brushes)
hat:    ..x...x...x...x.    ticks [240, 720, 1200, 1680]  (ride on 2-and-4 upbeats)
```

## Grid Notes

Subdivision policy: melody may use 8ths, triplets written as 16th approximations,
and syncopated entrances on any step; bass walks quarter notes (480-tick spacing).
Swing offsets: offbeat eighth positions (ticks +240 within each beat) are delayed
by +200 ticks before writing, so an offbeat at tick 240 lands at 440.
