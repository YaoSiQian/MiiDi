# Harmony Vocabulary

Seventh chords are the default quality. Core symbols: Cmaj7, Dm7, Em7, Fmaj7,
G7, A7, Bm7b5, E7, plus colors C6, Am6, Gsus4, and tritone-sub Db7.

## Characteristic Progressions

- **Major ii-V-I**: Dm7 - G7 - Cmaj7
- **Minor ii-V-i**: Bm7b5 - E7 - Am7
- **Rhythm-changes turnaround**: C6 - Am7 - Dm7 - G7
- **Tritone-sub cadence**: Db7 - Cmaj7

## Cadences

Resolve with the ii-V-I as the default authentic close; delay arrival by swapping
G7 for Db7 (tritone substitution) before Cmaj7. End minor tunes on Am6 or Am7
rather than a bare triad, and use Bm7b5 - E7 to turn any section back toward A
minor.
