# Instrumentation

| role | GM program | name | register note |
|---|---|---|---|
| lead melody | 56 | Trumpet | G4-G6, brassy horn line above the band |
| harmonic role | 4 | Electric Piano 1 (Rhodes) | C3-B4, rootless voicings mid-register |
| bass | 32 | Acoustic Bass | E1-G3, walking quarter notes |
| secondary horn | 66 | Tenor Sax | C3-F5, harmony lines and soli answers |

Avoid guitar-center shredding, dense pad stacks, and drum-machine stiffness: the
swing feel must come from the written offsets, not from extra notes. Keep the
piano comping sparse (two-note shells), the trumpet out of its low muddy octave,
and never double the bass melody.
