# Instrumentation

| role | GM program | name | register note |
|---|---|---|---|
| lead melody | 56 | Trumpet | C5-A6, soaring fast lead above everything |
| harmonic role | 0 | Acoustic Grand Piano | A2-A6, driving arpeggios and octaves |
| bass | 38 | Synth Bass 1 | E1-C3, locked 8th-note drive |
| support lead | 81 | Lead 2 (sawtooth) | C4-C6, doubles trumpet at chorus |

Avoid mellow pads, lazy tempos, and empty space in the chorus: energy must stay
high from first bar to last. No swing, no laid-back placement — the trumpet sits
on top of a relentlessly straight grid, and the piano never stops moving.
