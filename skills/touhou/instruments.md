## Role Groupings

**Harmonic bed** -- ZUN layers 3-5 of these simultaneously for the full chord pad:
strings (48) + harpsichord (6) + piano (0 or 1) + organ (16) + guitar (24). Each
voice moves independently but they share the same register and function.

**Lead melody** -- Square (80) and saw (81) leads carry the primary melody in
verses and choruses. Trumpet (56) appears in ~20% of files for the most exposed
melodic lines. Leads sit above the harmonic bed, typically C4-C8.

**Bass** -- Synth bass (36) dominates with locked 8th-note drive in the low
register. Slap bass (37) doubles or alternates. Upright bass (32) appears in
slower or acoustic-leaning pieces. All bass voices stay below C5.

**Accent** -- Celesta (8) provides high-register sparkle and counter-melody in
42 files. Voice Oohs (87) and Soprano Sax (66) appear in fewer pieces for
coloristic contrast.

## Multi-Program Layering

The defining trait of ZUN's orchestration: no single program carries the harmony
alone. The harmonic bed is always a stack of 3+ timbres playing the same chord
tones with independent voice leading. This creates the dense, bright wall of
sound characteristic of Touhou — removal of any one layer thins the texture
noticeably.
