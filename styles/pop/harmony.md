# Harmony Vocabulary

Diatonic major/minor vocabulary. Core symbols: C, Dm7, Em7, F, G, Am, G7, and
secondary colors Bm7b5, Csus4, Fadd9. Seventh chords appear mainly on the ii and
iii degrees; everything else is triad-first.

## Characteristic Progressions

- **Axis loop (I-V-vi-IV)**: C - G - Am - F
- **Sensitive female progression (vi-IV-I-V)**: Am - F - C - G
- **Pre-chorus lift**: Fmaj7 - G - Am7 - G7

## Cadences

Land section endings on V - I or F - C for a full resolution; use G7 to sharpen
the pull home. For a softer, modern ending, resolve Am7 - Fadd9 - C instead of a
hard authentic close.
