# Rhythmic Feel

Steady 4/4 backbeat at 84-132 BPM, quantized straight to the ppq=480 grid with no
swing offsets. Kick anchors beats 1 and 3, snare cracks beats 2 and 4, hats keep
even eighth notes.

## Drum Patterns

One bar = 1920 ticks; grid steps are 16ths (120 ticks each).

```
step:   1e&u2e&u3e&u4eu  (step = tick/120)
kick:   x.......x.......    ticks [0, 960]
snare:  ....x.......x...    ticks [480, 1440]
hat:    x.x.x.x.x.x.x.x.    ticks [0, 240, 480, 720, 960, 1200, 1440, 1680]
```

## Grid Notes

Subdivision policy: melody may use 8th/16th notes and occasional syncopations that
start on any 16th step; bass lands mostly on beat downbeats with passing 8ths;
comping hits land on 8th positions. No swing offsets — everything sits exactly on
the integer-tick grid.
